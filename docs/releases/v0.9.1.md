# Elora 0.9.1 Beta

The first big update: besides multiplayer there is now **“The Silent Springs”**, an
adventure just for you – as a preview with a prologue and three chapters.

## Download

| System | File | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Linux | `elora-0.9.1-linux-x86_64.AppImage` or `.tar.gz` | AppImage: make it executable and start it |
| Windows | `elora-0.9.1-windows-x86_64.zip` | extract, start `elora.exe` |
| macOS (Apple Silicon) | `elora-0.9.1-macos-aarch64.dmg` | not signed: on the first start **right click → Open** |

Intel Macs are not built yet.

## New: the adventure (preview)

In the main menu under **Adventure**, with three save slots. The springs of the Tauland fall silent, the
colours fade from the village of Tauwinkel – Elora sets out to find out why.

- **Prologue – Tauwinkel:** the village with Oma Pfütze, Tüftel, Klonk, Lotte and Pip; learn hook, hammer and
  double jump
- **Chapter 1 – Blütenwiesen:** beekeeper Wabe, lost bees and the Bumblebear
- **Chapter 2 – Murmelwald:** Plumm the Owl, a mushroom child as a companion, memory runes and the
  Root Warden
- **Chapter 3 – Glutsandwüste:** Sirup's caravan, an oasis in need, ruins with secrets,
  quicksand, heat and the Sand Serpent
- **Abilities** from every freed spring: hook jerk, pull hook, stomp – and with them new paths
  in the old areas
- **Role-playing:** levels, skill tree, equipment, shops, weapon upgrades at Klonk, quests
  and dialogues with answers
- **Celebrations** in the village after every spring – Tauwinkel becomes more colourful with each spring
- **Music** per area and for every guardian, babble sounds for all characters

Chapter 4 (Frostspitzen), chapter 5 (Sternschlucht) and the finale follow in the next versions.

## New in multiplayer and everywhere

- **Smoother online:** your own character and camera without stutter, time sync without jumps
- **Main menu** with a lively Tauwinkel depending on the time of day, menu music and click sounds
- **Switching weapons on pickup** configurable (off, new weapons only, always)
- **New training map** (120 × 40) with all the new features
- **Map editor:** tool for adventure maps with characters, chests, switches, doors and transitions
- Page **“About Elora”** in the settings with licences and sources
- The window starts maximised; the map list when creating a server wraps cleanly

## Compatibility

Same protocol version as 0.9.0: players with 0.9.0 and 0.9.1 can play together.

## Known issues

- The adventure is a preview: balancing and maps will be adjusted after the first
  feedback.
- Servers behind DS-Lite can only be reached by players with IPv6.
