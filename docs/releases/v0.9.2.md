# Elora 0.9.2 Beta

Chapter 4 of the adventure, weather everywhere – and a fix that matters: **0.9.1 could not start
from its download packages** (the start map was missing). Please use 0.9.2.

## Download

| System | File | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Linux | `elora-0.9.2-linux-x86_64.AppImage` or `.tar.gz` | AppImage: make it executable and start it |
| Windows | `elora-0.9.2-windows-x86_64.zip` | **extract the whole ZIP** (right click → “Extract all”), then start `elora.exe` from the extracted folder |
| macOS (Apple Silicon) | `elora-0.9.2-macos-aarch64.dmg` | not signed: on the first start **right click → Open** |

Intel Macs are not built yet.

## Fixed

- **Packages start again:** the training map Elora starts with was missing from every 0.9.1
  package. On Windows (no console) this looked like nothing happening at all. A test now makes
  sure the start map is always shipped.
- **Errors are never silent anymore:** every start writes `elora.log`; a crash or startup error
  writes `crash.txt` (on Windows it opens in Notepad). Both lie next to the settings
  (`%APPDATA%\Elora`, `~/.config/elora`, `~/Library/Application Support/Elora`). Missing game data
  gets a clear message.
- **Older and weak graphics cards:** Elora falls back to the software renderer when no hardware
  adapter is found and only asks for what weak GPUs offer.
- No shot when entering a map with the grenade launcher selected; victory confetti across the
  whole screen; leaves and snow fall evenly while running and jumping; the cat sits on Pip's tree
  house.

## New in the adventure

- **Chapter 4 – Frostspitzen:** a mountain path out of Tauwinkel (stomp through the ice lid),
  the glacier foot, an abandoned mountain village with mountain guide **Flocke**, the summit ridge
  in a blizzard and the ice hall of **Ice Queen Kristella**.
- **Ice grip** (climbing claws) from Flocke in the middle of the chapter – later strengthened, so
  Elora can pull herself up climbing walls. New climbing spots in chapters 1–3 reward going back.
- **Cold:** a cold bar fills outside and twice as fast in a blizzard; fires and roofs warm Elora up.
- **New terrain and enemies:** thin ice over ice water, falling icicles, avalanches, snowball
  seals, ice bats and frost ghosts that freeze Elora for a moment.
- **Side quests:** lost climbers and eight clear crystals for Klonk (choose a pendant).
- **Music and sounds** for the mountains and Kristella (CC0).
- Elora gets the **hammer from Klonk** in the prologue instead of carrying it from the start.
- **Chests** open with sparkles and a sound.

## New: weather

- Rain, thunderstorm, fog, wind with leaves or petals, sandstorm, snow and blizzard – with
  particles, colour grading, lightning and an ambient sound track (CC0 recordings).
- **In the adventure** the weather follows the story and chance, and it matters: wind pushes Elora
  in the air and bends grenades, wet ground is slippery, lightning strikes after a warning, fog
  shortens the view.
- **Multiplayer maps:** `dm-winter` with snow, `ctf-nacht` with light fog, `dm-wueste` with wind
  and sand – atmosphere only, no gameplay effect.
- Setting **Graphics → Weather: full, gentle, off**.

## Compatibility

- **Protocol version stays 6:** 0.9.1 and 0.9.2 play together.
- Save games and your own maps from 0.9.1 keep working.
