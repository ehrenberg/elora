# World book: “The Silent Springs”

Status: **outline accepted** (E-210 to E-214) · Basis: E-203, E-206, E-207 · Details to follow

## 1. In one sentence

When the springs of the Tauland fall silent one after another and the colours fade from the village of Tauwinkel, Elora sets out to find out who is silencing the springs – and discovers that the village itself has forgotten something important.

## 2. The world: the Tauland

The drop creatures live on the **dew of the springs**: it gives them colour, courage and vitality. Five springs are spread across the land, each with its own colour and its own **guardian** – a large creature that watches over it.

| Spring | Colour | Area | Guardian | Gift of the spring |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blossom Spring | Green | Blütenwiesen | Bumblebear | Life – heals and makes things grow |
| Moss Spring | Brown | Murmelwald | Root Warden | Memory – preserves stories |
| Ember Spring | Gold | Glutsandwüste | Sand Serpent | Warmth – gives strength |
| Frost Spring | Ice blue | Frostspitzen | Ice Queen Kristella | Calm – clears thoughts |
| Star Spring | Violet | Sternschlucht | (none – the spring has vanished) | Dreams |

**The secret:** there once was a **sixth spring**, the **Origin Spring** – exactly where the village well of Tauwinkel stands today. Its guardian was forgotten when the founders built the village on top of it and sealed the well with a lid. His loneliness turned him into **the Withered One**: he wanders the land and drinks the colours of the other springs because he believes nobody needs him any more.

**The guardians are not villains.** Since their springs fell silent, they are confused and irritable. The fights against them are real boss fights; the story tells them as **calming** – afterwards the guardians help Elora. The whole adventure stays **playable for ages 12+**: no depiction of blood or cruelty (E-211).

## 3. Tauwinkel (hub)

A small village in a hollow, built around the sealed well. With every freed spring a colour returns – houses, flowers and villagers become visibly more colourful (the village shows the progress).

## 4. The areas

Each area is one connected map with several sections. The guardian waits at the end. After the fight Elora brings back a **spring spark**; Tüftel turns it into a new ability.

### 4.1 Blütenwiesen (meadow, earth)
- **Mood:** introduction, sunny, gentle hills, first hook spots, platforms.
- **Enemies:** spike beetles (walk back and forth), pollen blowers (shoot slow projectiles), grass hoppers (jump at Elora).
- **NPC:** Beekeeper Wabe – misses her bees.
- **Side quests:** find five bees; Pip's glitter stone.
- **Guardian: Bumblebear** – flies in circles, dives down; Elora has to hook onto blossoms to stay up.
- **New ability: hook jerk** – the hook pulls much faster (for long distances).

### 4.2 Murmelwald (forest, earth)
- **Mood:** tall trees, treehouses, lots of verticality, shady; whispering roots tell fragments of the old story.
- **Enemies:** root snakes (come out of the ground), squirrel pirates (throw nuts), mushroom imps (briefly poison).
- **NPC:** the old owl Plumm – collects stories.
- **Side quests:** collect memory runes (they tell of the Origin Spring!); bring a lost mushroom child home.
- **Guardian: Root Warden** – huge, blocks paths with roots; Elora hooks cores out of his bark.
- **New ability: pull hook** – the hook pulls objects, switches and small enemies towards Elora.

### 4.3 Glutsandwüste (desert, sand)
- **Mood:** shimmering heat, conveyor belts of quicksand, ruins of an ancient people, spike pits.
- **Enemies:** sand crabs (armoured, vulnerable from above), dune worms, spark moths.
- **NPC:** Sirup the caravan leader – offers rare goods.
- **Side quests:** bring water to an oasis; dig out a buried ruin.
- **Guardian: Sand Serpent** – dives under the sand; Elora has to hit it with a stomp from the air when it surfaces.
- **New ability: stomp** – thrust downwards from the air: breaks floors, stuns enemies.

### 4.4 Frostspitzen (winter, snow, ice)
- **Mood:** steep peaks, ice surfaces, jump-pad shafts, blizzard sections, an abandoned mountain village.
- **Enemies:** snowball seals, ice-spike bats, frost ghosts.
- **NPC:** Flocke the mountain guide – wants to reach the summit.
- **Side quests:** rescue lost climbers; ice crystals for Klonk.
- **Guardian: Ice Queen Kristella** – freezes floors to ice; Elora has to hold on to walls.
- **New ability: ice grip** – briefly cling to walls and jump off them.

### 4.5 Sternschlucht (night, stone)
- **Mood:** dark rocks, stars, glowing mushrooms, floating islands, the ruins of the vanished Star Spring.
- **Enemies:** shadow moths, stone goblins, echoes (mirror images of Elora).
- **NPC:** Luma the star weaver – last guardian of the Star Spring, grown weak.
- **Side quests:** collect star fragments; piece together Luma's song.
- **Event:** here Elora meets **the Withered One** for the first time – he takes the last colour from the Star Spring and disappears towards Tauwinkel.
- **New ability: glide** – fall slowly in the air and drift far.

### 4.6 Finale: the old well (Tauwinkel)
- The Withered One returns to where he came from: beneath the village. Elora opens the well and descends into the **Origin Cave**.
- **The Withered One** – not a fight to defeat him: Elora has to use all abilities to bring the five colours to him (hook jerk, pull hook, stomp, ice grip, glide).
- **Ending:** the Withered One becomes the guardian of the Origin Spring again; the well is opened, the village celebrates, Oma Pfütze remembers the forgotten song.

## 6. Role-playing elements (sketch – details open, O-204)

- **Levels:** experience from enemies and quests; each level gives a **dewdrop point** for the skill tree and a little more health.
- **Skill tree** at Tüftel's, three branches:
  - *Movement* – improvements to the area abilities (e.g. longer gliding, stronger stomp)
  - *Combat* – more damage, shorter cooldowns, knockback
  - *Spring* – health, armour, healing from plants
- **Weapon upgrades** at Klonk's with materials from the areas (amber, ice crystal, ember stone …): hammer (impact, reach), grenade launcher (explosion size, shrapnel), laser (bounce, pierce). Later **new weapons** (e.g. dew sling, frost bow).
- **Equipment & loot:** hat, cloak, boots, pendant with small bonuses; rare finds from bosses. **Currency: gleam drops.**
- **Quests & dialogues:** main quests from Oma Pfütze, side quests on the notice board and from NPCs; dialogues with choices (friendly/curious/cheeky) that change the tone and small rewards.
- **Principle:** hook, jump and double jump feel like in multiplayer; new abilities are added, base values do not change.

## 7. PvP mode “Quellenkampf” (sketch)

Teams defend their spring and capture the opponents' one; drops from defeated enemies and springs grant **levels during the match**, and at a spring stone you choose small upgrades (damage, health, an area ability). Everything resets after the round (E-204).

## 8. Play time (proposal for E-214)

Goal: **main story about 6–8 hours, 12 hours and more with side content.**

| Building block | Scope | Play time (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Prologue + Tauwinkel | Tutorial, village, first quests | 20–30 min |
| Per area (5×) | 3–4 sections (separate maps) + guardian arena; intermediate save points | 60–90 min main path |
| Finale | Origin Cave, multi-stage final fight | 30–45 min |
| Side quests | about 25 (4–5 per area + village), some with consequences from dialogues | 3–4 h |
| Collectibles | Memory runes, glitter stones, bees …, hidden behind later abilities (returning pays off) | 1–2 h |
| After the finale | **Spring trials**: harder versions of the areas and guardians, time trials per section | open |

That adds up to about **20 adventure maps** plus village and arenas. To keep this feasible, the maps are built like the release maps with the tile kit and editor, and enemies, NPCs, quests and dialogues are placed **in the editor** (O-206).
